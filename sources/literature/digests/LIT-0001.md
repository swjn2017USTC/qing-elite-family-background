## THESIS
The paper introduces the authors' approach to the nominative linkage of records of Qing officials contained in the China Government Employee Datasets-Qing (CGED-Q), specifically the Jinshenlu (JSL) and the Examination Records (ER), and reports an assessment of the attributes available in the original sources for the purpose of disambiguation.

## PRIMARY MATERIALS USED
- Records of Qing officials included in the CGED-Q Jinshenlu (JSL) and Examination Records (ER).
- Quarterly rosters of civil and military officials produced by the government and by commercial presses, used to construct the datasets.
- Records of examination degree holders, used to construct the datasets.

## METHOD / INFERENCE
The abstract describes an assessment procedure rather than a statistical model: each primary attribute available in the original sources is assessed for its usefulness in disambiguation, focusing on diversity and potential for inconsistent recording. For officials not affiliated with the Eight Banners, the primary attributes assessed are surname, given name, and province and county of origin; the small subset of officials affiliated with the Bannermen is assessed separately. Secondary attributes available in the data that may be useful for adjudicating candidate matches are also assessed, after which the authors describe the approach they developed that addresses the identified issues with the primary and secondary attributes. No algorithmic, software, or parameter details are given in the abstract.
