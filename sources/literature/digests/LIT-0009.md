## THESIS
Based on a specific analysis of one person's Zhu-juan in the late Qing Dynasty, the paper explains and annotates Tong Shaowen's Zhu-juan written for a provincial examination, in order to highlight its historical, cultural, and historical-data value and to provide a reference for the study of imperial examination history, local history, and family history.

## PRIMARY MATERIALS USED
- Tong Shaowen's Zhu-juan (red-ink copy of test papers used in imperial examinations), identified as his Zhejiang provincial examination paper written in 1846.
- The Collection of Zhu-juan in the Qing Dynasty, compiled by Gu Tinglong and published by Taipei Chengwen Publishing House in 1992, stated as the origin of Tong Shaowen's Zhu-juan.
- The Huanggang Tong's Genealogy (1949) from Hongnishan Village, Nubu Street, Lanxi City, Zhejiang Province, used in combination with the Zhu-juan for annotation.

## METHOD / INFERENCE
The abstract describes a specific analysis of the Zhu-juan and an annotation of it. The Zhu-juan is described as including the personal resume, teacher-student relationship, the content and answers of the examination questions, and the examiner's comments. The annotation is stated to combine the Zhu-juan with the Huanggang Tong's Genealogy (1949) and to cover the personal resume, the teacher-student relationship, and the political ideas. No further method detail is given in the abstract.
