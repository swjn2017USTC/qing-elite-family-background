## THESIS
The paper is a review of historical census record linkage: for the past 80 years social scientists have been linking historical censuses across time to study economic and geographic mobility, and the volume of such linkage has recently expanded greatly, which the paper surveys along with its methods and problems.

## PRIMARY MATERIALS USED
- Historical censuses, linked across time.
- Machine-readable data created by genealogical organizations, identified as the main driver of the recent expansion in historical census record linkage.
